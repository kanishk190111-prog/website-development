About Rohit Suresh Saraf

A little bit about me

Rohit Suresh Saraf is a popular Indian actor known for his work in Hindi films, the Netflix series [Mismatched], and the movie [Ishq Vishk Rebound]. Born on December 8, 1996, in Kathmandu, Nepal, he emerged as a major youth icon in Indian entertainment.


The history of acting began in 534 BC, when Thespis stepped onto a stage to play a character, paving the way for screen legends like Al Pacino and transformative performances of real historical figures.
